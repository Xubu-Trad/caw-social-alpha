02 / PRINCIPLES

Built for participation.
Reviewed in public.

The manifesto and recovered R2 are the starting point. These are design requirements to prove, not promises earned by publishing a repository.

[ 01 ]

More than
one entrance.

Portable clients, alternate hosts and usable records. A domain can be a shortcut; it cannot be the only way in.

[ 02 ]

No exclusive
switch.

R2 rejects developer backdoors, proxies and multisigs. Code, dependencies and deployed authority still need independent review.

[ 03 ]

Recover the
actual record.

Operators must be able to restore accounts, actions and required content. A hash alone cannot supply a missing file.

Availability, storage and funding can fail. We will publish specific tests and their limits. CAW activity is pseudonymous and can still be linked.

03 / OPEN REVIEW

Read it.
Reproduce it.
Question it.

Follow the source, examine the evidence, and keep failures visible. Community agreement and production readiness have not been established.
